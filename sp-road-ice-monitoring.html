Road-ice monitoring with sensors on buses

BachelorOpen topic

HES-SO Valais-Wallis or Università di Torino · Bachelor thesis

Icy roads are a major winter risk in mountain regions. Public buses travel the same roads every day and can carry sensors that measure road-surface conditions along their routes.

This project builds and evaluates a first version of such a system, sending the measurements over low-power wide-area networks (LoRaWAN or NB-IoT) and turning them into a map of road conditions.

What you will do

  • Choose sensors and an IoT connectivity option (LoRaWAN or NB-IoT)
  • Collect and clean data along bus routes
  • Visualise road conditions and assess coverage, delay and energy use

Background

Interest in IoT and data analysis; programming in Python.

Research theme: Opportunistic and resilient communication
