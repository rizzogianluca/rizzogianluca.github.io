Energy-aware orchestration and digital twins for sustainable 6G networks

PhDOngoing

PhD thesis of Berk Buzcu · HES-SO Valais-Wallis, within UNITY-6G

This PhD studies how to orchestrate 6G networks spanning several technological domains so that they use less energy, and how digital twins of the network can support these decisions. It is carried out within the Horizon Europe project UNITY-6G.

What the project covers

  • Energy-aware multi-domain orchestration architectures
  • Digital twins for intent-driven, sustainable network management
