LEO satellites for emergency communications

MasterOpen topic

Università di Torino · Master thesis

Low-Earth-orbit (LEO) satellite constellations can provide connectivity when ground networks fail. This project adapts stochastic geometry models developed for energy-efficient cellular networks to LEO networks, to choose transmit powers that save energy while guaranteeing service to first responders.

What you will do

  • Model a LEO constellation and its users with stochastic geometry
  • Derive coverage and energy metrics
  • Optimise transmit power under service constraints

Background

Probability; interest in wireless communications.

Related publications
