Short bio

Gianluca Rizzo is Associate Professor of Computer Science at the Università di Torino, Italy, and Senior Research Associate at the University of Applied Sciences of Western Switzerland (HES-SO Valais-Wallis). He received his PhD in Computer Science from EPFL in 2008 and his MSc in Electronics Engineering from Politecnico di Torino in 2001. He was a researcher at IMDEA Networks in Madrid (2009–2013), worked at ABB and Telecom Italia Lab, and was Associate Professor at the University of Foggia (2020–2024). His research focuses on the design and performance evaluation of distributed and wireless systems, including sustainable 6G networks, mobile base stations and decentralised learning at the network edge. He is a TPC lead member of IEEE INFOCOM, Area Editor of Elsevier Computer Communications, and an IEEE Senior Member.
