Age of information in IoT networks

MasterOpen topic

Università di Torino · Master thesis

In many IoT applications what matters is not only whether information arrives, but how recent it is. This project develops mean-field models of the age of information when updates spread through a large IoT network by gossip, and uses them to tune the protocol.

What you will do

  • Model gossip-based dissemination in a large IoT network
  • Derive mean-field approximations of the age of information
  • Validate them by simulation and use them to optimise the protocol

Background

Probability and stochastic processes; programming for simulation.
