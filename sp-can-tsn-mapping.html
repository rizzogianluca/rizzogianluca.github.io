Mapping CAN/CAN-FD in-vehicle traffic onto Time-Sensitive Networking

BachelorOngoing

Università di Torino · internship and Bachelor thesis

Vehicles are moving from CAN and CAN-FD buses to Ethernet-based Time-Sensitive Networking (TSN). This project studies how CAN/CAN-FD traffic can be mapped onto TSN, and back, while preserving its timing requirements.

The work starts with a gap analysis between the two technologies and continues with a mapping and its evaluation.

What the project covers

  • Analyse the timing requirements of CAN/CAN-FD traffic
  • Compare them with the mechanisms offered by TSN
  • Design and evaluate a mapping between the two
