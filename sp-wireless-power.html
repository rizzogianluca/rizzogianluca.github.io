Wireless power for vehicles and IoT

MasterOpen topic

Università di Torino · Master thesis

Simultaneous wireless information and power transfer (SWIPT) lets networks power small devices over the air. This project assesses where wireless power transfer is worth it, for battery-less IoT sensors and for vehicles, in terms of coverage, energy and cost.

What you will do

  • Model a network that delivers both data and power
  • Evaluate coverage and energy efficiency
  • Identify configurations and scenarios where it pays off

Background

Probability; interest in wireless networks.
