Communication-efficient federated and gossip learning

MasterOpen topic

Università di Torino · Master thesis

Exchanging full models is the main cost of federated and gossip learning on mobile devices. This project studies compression of models and updates, and when it reduces traffic and energy without hurting accuracy.

What you will do

  • Survey compression techniques for distributed learning
  • Integrate them into a federated or gossip learning framework
  • Measure the trade-off between accuracy, traffic and energy

Background

Machine learning; programming in Python.
