Gossip learning on real devices

BachelorOpen topic

Università di Torino or HES-SO Valais-Wallis · Bachelor thesis

In gossip learning, devices train machine-learning models together by exchanging models with their neighbours, without a central server. Most results so far come from simulations.

This project implements gossip learning on real devices and measures what happens in practice.

What you will do

  • Implement a gossip learning protocol on phones or Raspberry Pis
  • Run experiments with a simple learning task
  • Measure accuracy, energy consumption and network traffic

Background

Programming in Python; basic machine learning.
