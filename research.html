Research

My work is about designing networked systems that do more with less: less energy, less infrastructure, less coordination. I combine analytical performance models with optimisation and learning, and validate the results with simulations, testbeds and prototypes.

  • Sustainable 6G networks
  • Moving networks
  • Decentralised learning at the edge
  • Multi-agent coordination
  • Opportunistic and resilient communication
  • Performance modelling methods

Sustainable 6G networks

Energy-aware orchestration of multi-domain 6G networks, network sharing between operators, and simultaneous wireless information and power transfer (SWIPT) for battery-less IoT devices.

Projects: UNITY-6G

Moving networks

Industry collaboration

Since 2009 I have worked as a technical consultant for the avionic and utility-communications industry in Switzerland, on the modelling, analysis and validation of critical communication systems and deterministic networks. Earlier I designed utility communication networks at ABB and optical-network resilience solutions at Telecom Italia Lab, work that led to a patent granted in the US and Europe.
