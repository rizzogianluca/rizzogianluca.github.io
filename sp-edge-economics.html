Economics of edge computing

MasterOpen topic

Università di Torino · Master thesis

Edge computing brings computation close to users, but deploying it everywhere is expensive. This project builds cost and demand models to decide where and when operators should deploy edge (MEC) resources.

What you will do

  • Model demand for edge services and deployment costs
  • Formulate the deployment decision as an optimisation problem
  • Evaluate strategies in realistic scenarios

Background

Optimisation or economics of networks; programming.
