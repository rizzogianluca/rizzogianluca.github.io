Handovers at scale

MasterOpen topic

Università di Torino · Master thesis

In dense networks with many mobile users, handovers between cells become frequent and costly. This project uses mean-field approximations to compute handover rates efficiently in large networks and to study how network design affects them.

What you will do

  • Model user mobility and cell layout
  • Develop a mean-field approximation of handover rates
  • Compare it with simulation and use it to evaluate network configurations

Background

Probability and stochastic processes; programming for simulation.

Related publications
