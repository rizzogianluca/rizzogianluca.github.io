Opportunistic information sharing for emergencies

BachelorOpen topic

Università di Torino or HES-SO Valais-Wallis · Bachelor thesis

After a disaster, the mobile network is often unavailable exactly when people need information most. Devices can still exchange messages directly with those nearby, so that information "floats" in the area where it is relevant.

This project builds or evaluates an app based on this idea, building on our earlier Floater prototype.

What you will do

  • Study the Floating Content approach and existing prototypes
  • Implement or extend an app for device-to-device sharing
  • Evaluate it in experiments or simulations

Background

Mobile or network programming.
