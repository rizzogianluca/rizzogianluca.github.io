Carbon- and cost-aware energy accounting for edge data centres

BachelorOngoing

Università di Torino · internship and thesis, with an industrial partner of UNITY-6G · starting

Edge data centres draw power from distribution grids whose carbon intensity changes from place to place and hour to hour. This project asks how much CO₂ and money energy-saving mechanisms for edge computing really save when energy is valued at the local (locational) carbon intensity of the grid rather than at a national average.

It combines carbon flow tracing in distribution grids with live energy measurements from a Kubernetes cluster.

What the project covers

  • Compute hourly carbon-intensity profiles at the grid node hosting an edge site, using carbon flow tracing
  • Build a service that converts measured energy into cost and CO₂ in near real time
  • Compare average and locational carbon accounting, and the effect of shifting deferrable workloads in time
