Student projects

I supervise Bachelor's and Master's theses, semester projects and internships at the Università di Torino and at HES-SO Valais-Wallis, and PhD students in networking and distributed systems. Projects are linked to my research and often to ongoing funded projects with European partners.

Example topics

Each topic can be shaped into a Bachelor's, Master's or PhD project, with more emphasis on implementation or on modelling depending on your interests.

Moving networks

Simulate or optimise vehicles and drones acting as mobile base stations in a city: where should they go, and when do they save energy or capacity?

Sustainable 6G

Measure and reduce the energy footprint of 6G network functions and orchestration, from models to testbed experiments.

Wireless power for IoT

Model and evaluate networks that power battery-less IoT devices wirelessly (SWIPT), using stochastic geometry and simulation.

Learning without servers

Implement and evaluate gossip learning among phones or IoT devices, and study when cooperation between devices pays off.

Teams of autonomous agents

Design decentralised planning algorithms for groups of drones or robots that must keep working when some members fail.

Opportunistic communication

Build or analyse apps that share information locally between nearby devices, for example after a disaster when the network is down.

What you will do

  • Work on an open research question with a clear, achievable scope.
  • Combine analytical models, simulation, machine learning or prototyping, depending on the project.
  • Collaborate with PhD students and, where possible, with partners in European projects; strong theses can lead to a publication.

How to apply

Write to me by email with your CV, your transcript of records and a few lines on the topic or kind of work that interests you. Useful background: computer networks, probability, and programming in Python or C/C++.
