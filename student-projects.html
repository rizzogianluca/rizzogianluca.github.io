Bachelor projects

Bachelor's theses and final internships for students of Computer Science at the Università di Torino and of Business Information Technology at HES-SO Valais-Wallis. A Bachelor project typically focuses on implementation, simulation or measurement, with a clear and achievable goal for the duration of the thesis.

Master projects

Master's theses and semester projects for students of the Master's degree in Computer Science at the Università di Torino and for students from partner universities. A Master project tackles an open research question, combining a model or an algorithm with its evaluation; strong theses often lead to a publication at an international conference.

PhD projects

PhD positions are funded through research projects at the Università di Torino and at HES-SO Valais-Wallis, often jointly with partner universities. Candidates should hold (or be about to obtain) a Master's degree in computer science, electrical or telecommunications engineering, or a related field, with a solid background in networking, probability or machine learning. Open positions are announced on this site and on LinkedIn; spontaneous applications from strong candidates are welcome.

How to apply

Write to me by email with your CV, your transcript of records, the level of project you are looking for (Bachelor, Master or PhD) and a few lines on the topic or kind of work that interests you. Open topics are starting points: we shape the final project together. Useful background: computer networks, probability, and programming in Python or C/C++.
