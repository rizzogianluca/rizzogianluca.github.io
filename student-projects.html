Bachelor, Master and PhD projects

I supervise Bachelor's and Master's theses, semester projects and PhD students at the Università di Torino and at HES-SO Valais-Wallis. Projects are linked to my research and often to ongoing funded projects with European partners.

Bachelor projects

Bachelor's theses and final projects for students of Computer Science at the Università di Torino and of Business Information Technology at HES-SO Valais-Wallis. A Bachelor project typically focuses on implementation, simulation or measurement: building a prototype, extending a simulator, or running experiments on a testbed, with a clear and achievable goal for the duration of the thesis.

Master projects

Master's theses and semester projects for students of the Master's degree in Computer Science at the Università di Torino and for students from partner universities. A Master project tackles an open research question: it combines a model or an algorithm with its evaluation, and strong theses often lead to a publication at an international conference.

PhD projects

PhD positions are funded through research projects at the Università di Torino and at HES-SO Valais-Wallis, often in collaboration with European partners. Candidates should hold (or be about to obtain) a Master's degree in computer science, electrical or telecommunications engineering, or a related field, with a solid background in networking, probability or machine learning. Open positions are announced on this site and on LinkedIn; spontaneous applications from strong candidates are welcome.

Example topics

Each topic can be shaped to the level of the project, with more emphasis on implementation or on modelling depending on your interests.

Moving networks

Simulate or optimise vehicles and drones acting as mobile base stations in a city: where should they go, and when do they save energy or capacity?

Bachelor · Master · PhD

Sustainable 6G

Measure and reduce the energy footprint of 6G network functions and orchestration, from models to testbed experiments.

Master · PhD

Wireless power for IoT

Model and evaluate networks that power battery-less IoT devices wirelessly (SWIPT), using stochastic geometry and simulation.

Master · PhD

Learning without servers

Implement and evaluate gossip learning among phones or IoT devices, and study when cooperation between devices pays off.

Bachelor · Master · PhD

Teams of autonomous agents

Design decentralised planning algorithms for groups of drones or robots that must keep working when some members fail.

Master · PhD

Opportunistic communication

Build or analyse apps that share information locally between nearby devices, for example after a disaster when the network is down.

Bachelor · Master

How to apply

Write to me by email with your CV, your transcript of records, the level of project you are looking for (Bachelor, Master or PhD) and a few lines on the topic or kind of work that interests you. Useful background: computer networks, probability, and programming in Python or C/C++.
